# Marketing

## What this area is for
Getting the right runners to find and trust Lewis. This is mainly Instagram, plus the Squarespace website.

## Key facts
- **Instagram (@lewisthurlbourne) is the main source of clients:** 8 of the roster and 4 of 4 live leads (Oct 2026). It had 1,563 followers on 28 Oct 2026.
- **The account is Lewis's open training diary**, "the honest side of trying to run faster". It's documentary, not advice. Coaching is not sold on the grid.
- **Four pillars:** THE CHASE (his own training and races), UNDER THE HOOD (how he thinks, shown with his own data), THE PEOPLE (training partners), THE PERSON (life around running).
- **Upcoming races that anchor the content:** Malaga Half on 8 Nov 2026, FNUL 5K on 27 Nov 2026 (target 15:00), Valencia 10K in Jan 2027.

## Voice (short version)
State what he did, never what "you should" do. Plain openers, understated, specific numbers, one dry wink at most, British spelling, no emojis, no hype words (grind, journey, hack, unlock). Full rules are in `instagram-creative-brief.md` section 3.

## Tools
Instagram, Squarespace (website), DaVinci Resolve Studio (editing), Canva, and Notion (content plans and dated content in Tasks with Area = Content).

## Files
- `instagram-creative-brief.md` is Lewis's creative brief, and you must read it before making anything for Instagram. Its tool paths (`~/Downloads/...`) are on Lewis's Mac, not in this folder.
- `reel-formats-research-2026-10-29.md` is research on the top marathon reel formats and the 2025–26 algorithm, with three recommended trials.
- In Notion: "Instagram Growth & Coaching Funnel — 2026", "Instagram content plan — Threshold + race-distance methodology", Proof Bank, and "Offer + landing page copy (v1)".
