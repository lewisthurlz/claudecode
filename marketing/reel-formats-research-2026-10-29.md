# Marathon reel formats: research (29 Oct 2026)

Desk research by an agent. The format ranking is a judgement built from 2025–26 algorithm evidence and the running creators it could verify. No running-specific study exists. View counts were not verified, and Instagram itself couldn't be browsed.

## Ranked formats (fit for Lewis)
1. **Session breakdown.** 20–40s of the session, with rep splits on screen and the session as a headline hook ("3 x 5K at MP, 6 weeks out"). Drives saves and sends. Fit: high. Examples: Sweat Elite, Phily Bowden (2:25, coach).
2. **Episodic build-up series ("Week X of Y").** Same opening card, look and length each week, counting down to a race. Drives return viewers and follows. Fit: high, since build-up already beats results for him.
3. **Data or watch-stat overlay on a run clip** (animated pace, heart rate and splits). Drives watch time and rewatches. Fit: high. Avoid the stock COROS overlay (see the creative brief).
4. **Talking head while running or straight after.** 30–60s, one thought, raw. Drives follows and comments. Fit: high.
5. **Day in the life of a working sub-elite** (job, commute, doubles). Drives relatability and follows from time-goal amateurs. Fit: high.
6. **Short lab/test explainer** (lab carousel cut down to a Reel). Drives saves and follows. Fit: high.
7. **Race-day POV, "race in 30 seconds".** Drives reach. Fit: medium. Check race filming rules first (Matt Choi was banned by NYRR in 2024).
8. **Kit or shoe rotation for a block.** Drives saves and comments. Fit: medium, as it attracts kit-chasers.
9. **Mistake or lesson retrospective**, told in the first person. Drives shares. Fit: high, provided it isn't framed as "you should".
10. **Myth vs truth / "do this, not that".** Fit: low, because it's advice framing.
11. **Comedy, meme and trend-audio skits.** Fit: low, because they draw the wrong audience.

## Instagram algorithm, 2025–26
- **Top signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025):** watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers.
- **Originality (Apr 2026):** accounts that mostly repost others' content are removed from recommendations. Raw, original content is favoured (reported from Mosseri's Dec 2025 memo, via secondary sources).
- **Trial Reels:** shown only to non-followers and kept off the grid, so they're good for testing new formats.
- **"Your Algorithm" (Dec 2025):** users can pick the topics they see. Clear topic words ("marathon training") in captions and on-screen text help.
- **Metricool 2026 (24.3M posts):** Reels drive discovery, and carousels get about 9x the saves of single images.
- **Unverified:** the "first 3 seconds" rule, and "sends are worth 3–5x likes".

## Recommended trials
1. **Session breakdown Reel**, as Trial Reels.
2. **Build-up series** for the next A-race.
3. **Working sub-elite day in the life**, kept raw and phone-shot.

Run each 2–3 times over a month. Compare sends per reach and follows per reach against the weekly table reel.
