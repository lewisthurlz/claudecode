## 0. How to use this file

1. Read sections 1 to 4 every time. Read the one format playbook (section 5) that matches the ask.
2. Work from his idea, not from a template of ideas. He has done the creative thinking; the job is to build it to spec, fast, with his material.
3. One question maximum before building, and only if the answer changes the build. Otherwise build, state the assumption, and let him tweak.
4. Output is always the finished object (script, slide spec plus rendered PNGs, caption text, overlay command), never advice about the object.
5. Run the checklist in section 8 before handing anything over. Say which checks you could not run.
6. No emojis anywhere, including captions and on-screen text, unless he types them himself.

## 2. What the data says about creative choices

These are the reasons behind the rules in sections 3 and 4. Small samples; treat as evidence, not law.

- **The table is the reach engine.** Seven of seven weekly tables beat the median. 11 Oct and 19 Oct tables: 50K and 54K views, 26K reach each, 3 to 4 saves per 1,000 views. It is a 6 to 7 second loop that people read, save, rewatch and leave. Follows: about 1 per 1,000 reach. Comments: zero.
- **The face converts.** 16 Oct lab film (filmed by Lawrence Reeve, part one of the road to sub-15): 14.7K views, 300 saves (20 per 1,000), 53 comments, 116 shares, 54 follows (3.7 per 1,000 views, 5.2 per 1,000 reach). 14 Oct lab carousel: 59 comments. Both are dark, cool-toned, close on his face, and tell a story with his own numbers.
- **The grid is warm and the lab is cold.** Terracotta track, blue sky, orange shoes, daylight on 60-plus posts. The two lab posts are the only dark, teal-graded frames and they are the two that converted. Contrast inside the grid earns the second look.
- **Stock overlays are invisible.** 19 reels carry the COROS data overlay (10.18 km, 5.00 km, 3.00 km) and sit at baseline. They look like everyone's.
- **Only three posts have a title card** (sub-15 plan, lab carousel cover, 100 km breakdown). The rest of the grid reads as untitled action shots. The profile is a landing page for 98.6% strangers and most tiles say nothing.
- **Build-up beats result.** Race-result reels have the lowest saves; weeks before a race and the story around it carry the saves and the comments.
- **The honest failure post** (the failed session) has the best share rate on the account.
- **A plain story invitation** on 24 Oct: 43 link clicks from 407 views. No production.
- **Audience:** 84.5% men, over half 25 to 34, 60% UK and falling as the tables travel. Road runners, 5K to marathon. Track content underperforms.

## 3. Voice (how it sounds)

Full filter in `lewis-voice.md`. The working rules:

- **Documentary, not advice.** "What I did", never "what you should do". If a line could start with "you should", cut it or turn it into what he does.
- **Plain opener.** The first line is the fact a stranger would care about, said straight: "Week 12 of the London build: 130km." No "Recap", no "Let's talk about", no question-hook unless the question is real ("What's stopping me from running a sub 15 5K?").
- **Understated.** Wins stated flat, no hedging and no hype. "Not quite what we came for but it's the start of something." "Not bad for a small stocky runner."
- **Numbers do the talking.** Paces, kilometres, bpm, lactate. Always specific. "5 x 3k @ 3:29/km off 3' float", never "a hard session".
- **Dry wink.** Mock himself, never the knowledge. One per post at most.
- **Sentence-length rhythm.** Short declaratives. Line breaks between thoughts in captions. No bullet walls in captions.
- **Vocabulary he uses:** the build, the block, session, easy, steady, threshold, float, long run, marathon pace, structure, the road to sub 15, chasing.
- **Vocabulary he does not use:** grind, journey, unlock your potential, game-changer, secret, hack, "guys", "sign up", "link in bio" as a reflex.
- **CTA only where the funnel wants it.** Trial reels and framework reels carry a keyword ask (comment "structure" or "marathon"). Diary posts carry none, or "details in the caption". A story carries a plain invitation ("if you want the plan behind this, message me").
- **Captions end on a forward line**, not a sign-off: "This is the structure I used to run 2:31 at London." "Stay tuned." "Time to get the spikes on!"
- **British spelling.** Kilometres, programme, periodise.

## 4. Visual system (how it looks)

One system across every format so the grid reads as one author. Measurements are on a 1080-wide canvas.

### 4.1 Type
- **Posted reels use Arial Bold (make_overlay.py and framework_batch.py default). Inter Bold is the fallback for carousels and long text.** Bold for everything on screen; Regular only for long body text in carousels. Files: `~/.claude/skills/clean-cut/assets/fonts/Inter-Bold.ttf`, `Inter-Regular.ttf`. Fallback Helvetica Neue Bold, then Arial Bold. (His existing on-grid text is a heavy grotesk; Inter matches it and is already his Clean Cut caption font.)
- **Uppercase for titles, labels and table text.** Sentence case only for spoken-word captions on talking heads.
- **Tracking:** giant numbers tight (minus 2 to minus 6 px), small labels wide (plus 4 to plus 9 px), headlines neutral.
- **Sizes (1080 wide):** lead-in 36 to 40; headline 110 to 160 (three lines maximum); sub-line 36 to 40; carousel headline 64 to 72; carousel body 30 to 34 at line-height 1.35; big stat 220 to 330; table numbers 62; table labels 21 to 27; talking-head captions 50.
- **Line length:** never more than 24 characters a line on a headline, 44 on body.

### 4.2 Colour
- **White on his footage.** Text is white (#FFFFFF), secondary text grey (#8B8E96), over his own video or still dimmed 30 to 50%.
- **Cutaways and data cards are transparent overlays on his own footage**, never black cards: white graphics on his clip under the same flat dim as the table reels (0.30 to 0.46). Corrected 29 Oct 2026: the near-black full-frame card was Claude's invention in the first talking-head cut, not his look, and he rejected it.
- **No accent colour. White only.** Emphasis is by opacity (full white versus 0.76 or 0.5), size and position, exactly as on the weekly table and the framework reels. Corrected 29 Oct 2026: the orange #FF8A1F accent was Claude's own choice in the 26 to 27 Oct talking-head work and was carried into this brief as if it were his; none of his posted reels use a colour, and he asked why it was there.
- **Grade:** leave his phone footage as shot plus a touch of contrast (1.05) and saturation (1.10), the base look from the trial reels. The cool teal lab grade is reserved for lab and documentary pieces; it is the contrast post, not the default.
- **Never:** gradients of colour, drop shadows except the 0 1px 6px black at 60% used on tables, coloured text boxes, stock graphics, stickers in grid posts.

### 4.3 Backgrounds and framing
- **Every frame has him or his world in it.** Backgrounds are his own clips and stills (track, road, lab, group, kit), never stock and never flat except full-frame data cards inside a talking head.
- **Cover frame rule.** Face large and toward the lens when the job is follows or comments (lab cover). Wide action shot when the job is reach (table reels). Either way, every reel now gets a title card on its cover so the grid reads as a landing page.
- **Title-card grammar** (proven on the sub-15 plan and the lab cover): LEAD-IN small caps, HEADLINE giant, SUB-LINE small caps ending in an arrow. Left-aligned, bottom-left or top-left, 90 px from the edge. Example: HOW I PLAN TO RUN A / SUB 15 5K / IN 60 DAYS ... AND HOW MY PB WENT FROM 15:54 TO 15:10 THIS SUMMER.
- **Safe zones (9:16, 1080 x 1920):** keep text inside x 90 to 990 and y 250 to 1500. Instagram UI covers the bottom 420 px and the right 150 px; the top 250 px sits under the header.
- **Safe zones (4:5, 1080 x 1350):** 80 px margins all round; nothing within 120 px of the bottom (the caption preview).
- **To-camera framing:** phone, vertical, eye level, his face in the upper-middle third. In his current set-up his chin sits around y 1200 to 1430 of 1920 and the wall above his head (y 270 to 560) is empty, which is where overlays go, on dark plates because the wall is light. Captions sit on the dark sweatshirt at y 1450 to 1500.

### 4.4 Motion and pacing
- **Table reel:** one continuous clip of him running, 6 to 7 seconds (5 s and 7 s are being trialled), table fades up at once, loops. No cuts. Average play time 8 to 10 s means people loop it; keep it loopable.
- **Talking head:** cut on word boundaries every 4 to 5 s, alternating 100% and 113% zoom, hook segment at 100%. Full-frame dark cutaways keyed to the line being said (a number, a bar chart, three things). Retention is the metric; the first 3 seconds are the hook, said plainly.
- **Documentary (filmed by someone else):** letterboxed 2.39:1 inside 9:16, slow, teal-graded, small centred captions. Only when Lawrence or another filmmaker shoots it; not something to imitate on a phone.
- **Carousel:** static slides, one idea per slide, one graphic per slide, the last slide is the ask.

### 4.5 Audio
- **Table reels:** one song, consistent across the batch so the song is not a variable (control has been Radiohead, Everything In Its Right Place, from where the instrumental comes in).
- **Talking head:** his own voice, phone stereo track as recorded, two-pass loudnorm to minus 14 LUFS, true peak minus 1.5. No music under speech unless he asks.
- **Stories:** as shot.

### 4.6 Format specs at a glance

| Format | Canvas | Length | Text treatment | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly table reel | 1080x1920 | 6 to 7 s loop | centred white table, title above, DETAILS IN THE CAPTION below | none |
| Carousel | 1080x1350, 6 to 8 slides | static | cover title card; headline + body + one graphic per slide; last slide ask | one element |
| Talking head | 1080x1920, 30 fps | 60 to 90 s | 50 px sentence-case white captions, transparent white cutaway graphics over his footage | none |
| Race protocol posts | mixed | mixed | title cards on covers | one element |
| Story | 1080x1920 | 1 to 3 frames | one line of his voice, plain invitation | none |

## 5. Format playbooks (input to output contracts)

### 5.1 Weekly table reel (Sunday, THE CHASE)
**Input:** the week's days, sessions and kilometres (from COROS or intervals.icu), the title line, the clip to use. **Output:** a `weeks.json` entry, the overlay render command, the cover title, and a caption.

Build with the existing tool, do not rewrite it: `python3 ~/Downloads/london-trial-reels/make_overlay.py <week> --title "A WEEK OF TRAINING FOR A SUB 15 5K"` (reads `weeks.json` beside it; layout constants were measured from the 11 Oct reel; dim 0.30 to 0.35, text shadow on). Composite onto the clip with ffmpeg (`export PATH=/opt/homebrew/bin:$PATH`).

Table content rules: six or seven rows (Sunday REST DAY shown when the week has one, it is being trialled), day in caps with the session under it in small caps (EASY RUN, 5 X 6' (1' REST) + 10KM, LONG RUN), kilometres right-aligned as whole numbers unless a decimal is real, TOTAL row, bottom line DETAILS IN THE CAPTION or the on-screen ask when the reel is a trial.

Title line patterns that have worked: A WEEK OF TRAINING FOR A SUB 15 5K; A WEEK OF TRAINING AS A 2:31 MARATHON RUNNER (identity framing, trialled 26 Oct); 100KM TRAINING WEEK BREAKDOWN; A WEEK OF TRAINING: 5K RACE WEEK.

Caption: opener states the week and total, then only the key sessions with exact paces, then one line of context, then a forward line. Model: the Week 12 caption (`london-trial-reels/captions/W12_caption_C1.txt`). Pinned comment: one line of context or the ask.

### 5.2 Carousel (UNDER THE HOOD)
**Input:** his idea in a sentence, the numbers or data he wants to show, the photos or clips to sit behind it. **Output:** a slide-by-slide spec (lead-in, headline, body, graphic, footer) and, when image paths are given, rendered 1080x1350 PNGs via `~/Downloads/content-agent/tools/carousel/render_carousel.py`.

Structure that converted (14 Oct lab carousel, 59 comments):
1. Cover: his face, title-card grammar, question or claim in his own situation. WHAT'S STOPPING ME FROM RUNNING A SUB 15 5K? / MY LAB RESULTS AS A 15:10 5K RUNNER.
2. The headline finding first, with the big number. IT'S NOT MY VO2 MAX. 69.8.
3. What changed, as a chart. Slope chart May to Oct.
4 to 6. One factor per slide: a one-sentence definition in plain words, his number, the graphic, what he is doing about it. Lactate curve, economy bars, zone table.
7. What's next: timeline of the upcoming races, then the ask. FOLLOW THE ROAD TO SUB 15 / SAVE THIS POST IF YOU FOUND IT USEFUL.

Slide rules: one graphic per slide in white line art (gauge, slope, curve, bars, table, timeline), headline under 24 characters a line, body under 45 words, numbers always his. Every slide has a dimmed still of him behind it. The viewer should be able to read every slide in four seconds.

Caption: the story in five short paragraphs, a thank-you to the collaborator if there is one, no hashtags block.

### 5.3 Talking head (Tuesday, UNDER THE HOOD)
**Input:** the idea or a rough script, the hook he wants to test. **Output:** the script in his voice (60 to 90 s, 150 to 220 words), three hook options as one-line alternatives, a cutaway list keyed to lines (what full-frame card appears on which words), the caption, and the keyword if it is a funnel reel.

Script shape: hook (one sentence, the claim or the stake, 3 s), the three things or the one thing (each as what he does and why, with a number), the turn (where people get it wrong, said as what he used to do), the close (one line forward, then the ask only if it is a funnel reel).

Filming note for him: phone, eye level, one point, the current room is fine; record the body as short takes so the cut can be tight. Two minutes of filming is enough; the barrier is pressing record, not the edit.

Edit path: Resolve project via the DaVinci Resolve MCP (cut, zoom alternation, export clean master), then `~/Downloads/underachieving-reel/tools/burn_captions.py` for the 50 px captions and `graphics.html` plus `make_visuals.py` for the dark cutaway cards. Clean Cut (`/clean-cut`) is the quick route for a plain cut with captions. Gotchas are in memory: ffmpeg here has no libass or drawtext, Chrome headless needs retries under load, the M1 Air throttles.

### 5.4 Race protocol (every race, THE CHASE)
Five posts, one race: (1) the week-before table with the race in the title, (2) a to-camera or story the day before, one line on what he is going for, (3) the race-day story as it happens, (4) the result post within 24 h, result stated flat, the build in the caption, (5) the honest debrief within a week, to camera or carousel, what the numbers said. Build-up beats result, so the weight is on 1, 2 and 5.

### 5.5 Story (daily, THE PERSON and the funnel)
**Input:** what happened or what he wants to invite. **Output:** one line in his voice (under 15 words), the sticker or link to add, and whether it is a diary frame or an invitation frame. Fortnightly invitation frame: plain, no design, "if you want the structure behind this, message me" or the keyword.

### 5.6 Caption only
**Input:** the post and what it is for (reach, follow, funnel). **Output:** the caption to the rules in section 3, plus a one-line pinned comment.

### 5.7 Collab post (THE PEOPLE)
**Input:** the session and who was there. **Output:** the clip plan (them in frame, him in frame, one line of chat), the caption naming them in the first line, the collab-tag list. Needs their consent; athletes are "what we did", never "look at my clients".

Output: a 30 to 90 s film, 1080x1920 with the picture in a 4:3 band, mono or muted-colour LUT, grain, small Inter title card,
one-line captions in his voice (at most four, one orange), music under with clip sound at -18 dB, black end card with his name.

## 6. Openers and hooks that are his

Patterns pulled from his own best posts. Reuse the pattern, not the line.

- The plain state of play: "Been a rough last few weeks but I think we're finally back in business."
- The real question: "What's stopping me from running a sub 15 5K?"
- The plan with a number and a date: "How I plan to run a sub 15 5K in 60 days."
- The flat result: "FNUL 5K - 15:10. Sometimes you've just got to shut up and run."
- The identity line: "A week of training as a 2:31 marathon runner."
- The honest miss: "Not quite what we came for but it's the start of something."
- The claim he is willing to defend: "Volume is king. That's a hill I'm willing to die on."
- The inside look: "An inside look at what went down in the lab last weekend."

Not his: "Here's what nobody tells you", "Stop doing X", "POV:", "3 mistakes you're making", any "you" hook aimed at the viewer's failings except where he is describing his own past.

## 8. Pre-publish checklist

Run every item. Report the ones you cannot judge (audio, how it feels to him).

1. Stranger test: would someone who has never heard of him care within one second? Is the first line the fact, not a label?
2. Documentary test: no "you should". Everything is what he did or does.
3. Pillar: which of the four is this, and does the format match the pillar's job?
4. Visual: Arial Bold, white only on his footage (no accent colour anywhere), graphics transparent over his clip, inside the safe zones, cover has a title card.
5. Numbers: every number is his and exact. Paces as m:ss/km, distances in km.
6. Length: table 6 to 7 s, talking head 60 to 90 s, carousel 6 to 8 slides, story line under 15 words.
7. Caption: plain opener, forward last line, CTA only if this post is funnel, no hashtags block, no emojis unless he added them, British spelling.
8. Trial discipline: if it is a trial, exactly one variable changed versus the control and the control is named.
9. Consent: anyone else in frame or named has said yes.
10. Hand-over: file paths in full, what he still has to do (song, posting time, keyword switch in ManyChat).

## 9. Do not

- Do not invent a hook formula. Do not package the experience into a lesson.
- Do not over-produce. Phone, him, one point. Production is the filmmaker's job when there is one.
- Do not put tips, business talk or coaching sells on the grid.
- Do not add stickers, stock footage, flat colour cards or black cutaway cards, any accent colour, gradients, second fonts.
- Do not change the table reel's look except as a named trial variable.
- Do not use the COROS data overlay as the only text on a session clip; give it a title card or leave it clean.
- Do not nag about the bio. Do not plan around Chasing Air group plans unless he raises them.
- Do not build new tools for a one-off; use the kit in section 7.
