# Coaching (delivery)

## What this area is for
Everything involved in coaching the athletes: programming, adjusting training, communication, race planning and onboarding new athletes once they've paid.

## Key facts
- **Offer:** one-to-one online coaching, £99/month, rolling. Start and stop dates are flexible.
- **Roster:** 13 active athletes (Oct 2026), 2 more starting in November. Mostly marathoners; the full range is 5K to marathon.
- **Lewis is the only coach.**
- **Check-ins happen as needed.** There is no fixed weekly call.

## Coaching philosophy
Volume first, intensity second. Pace guides, effort rules. Boring consistency beats flashy one-offs.
- It's better to be consistently good than occasionally great. Do as much easy volume as possible and the least intensity necessary, within the athlete's constraints.
- The aerobic system matters most in distance running, and intensity gives diminishing returns.
- Training is sequenced General → Pre-specific → Specific → Taper → Race → Recovery. Plan backwards from the key workouts in the Specific phase.
- Prioritise the athlete's individual limiters and the demands of their race.
- The coach is a guide, not a dictator. The relationship rests on trust, communication and honesty.

## Delivery standards (from Notion Coach Dashboard)
1. Every key session gets a response within 24 hours (one line minimum), and athlete messages are acknowledged within the same window.
2. If an athlete gives poor or terrible feedback, Lewis talks to them the same day.
3. Daily 10-minute skim after the morning run, and action any red flags.
4. Sunday review block every week, where every athlete is looked at.
5. No athlete ever opens Final Surge to an empty week.
6. Every A-race gets a race-week strategy call.
7. New athletes get their assets within 1 hour of payment, and their plan is live before the agreed start date.

## Tools

- **WhatsApp:** athlete communication.
- **Notion:** Athlete Dashboard (a page per athlete, plus the Athlete One-Pager template), Upcoming Race Calendar, Team Rules, Notifications.

## Onboarding steps (after payment)
1. Invite the athlete to Final Surge.
2. Send the onboarding doc.
3. Build the plan and start training.
