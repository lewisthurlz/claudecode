# Plan: 13 to 20 athletes (draft, 29 Oct 2026)

Status: draft for Lewis to review. Nothing here has been decided yet.

## Where things stand (from Notion, 29 Oct)
- **Roster:** 13 active, with 2 more due to start in November, which makes 15. That leaves **5 more** to reach 20, plus however many leave in the meantime.
- **Funnel, 30 days to 19 Oct:** 3,400 profile views → 84 link taps (2.5%) → 4 form enquiries (about 5% of taps).
- **Leads CRM since July:** 16 leads, of which 4 converted, 7 were lost (mostly Instagram DMs that went quiet) and 8 are still open.
- **Open pipeline:** Dylan Ratje-Irving (call done 26 Oct), George Wareing, Ahmad Hall, William Nunes, Morgan Boon (knee, about 8 weeks out), Sarah Harrison (rehab), Lawrence Reeve, Emlyn (new, not yet contacted).
- **The pace now** is about 4–5 enquiries a month, with about 1 in 3 converting. That's roughly 1.5 new athletes a month, so 20 by about March.

## Why the timing matters
November to January is when runners pick a coach for spring marathons (London, Manchester and Paris in April). If the funnel is improved before December, it will catch the busiest buying window of the year.

## The plan, in order of payoff
1. **Close the open pipeline (this week).** There are 8 open leads, and every one needs a next step with a date. Two or three of them converting gets you to 17–18 with no new marketing at all. An agent can draft each follow-up for you to approve.
2. **Ask for referrals (November).** You already have the "Referral Ask" template, 13 athletes and good results (AB 3:45 → 3:06, Lans 2:45 → 2:39). Send it to the 4–5 happiest athletes, ideally just after a good race or a PB.
3. **Make the Instagram invitation weekly instead of fortnightly.** On 24 Oct a plain story invitation got 43 link clicks from 407 views, which beats any other route you have. One frame a week, with no production needed. It also fits your brief, which keeps coaching off the grid but allows story invitations.
4. **Fix the link → enquiry step before December.** About 95% of the people who tap the link don't enquire. Ship a *simple* first version of the Training Audit, as a Tally form plus a short page, rather than the full scoring engine. Add the 5-days-a-week qualifier and a source field so you know where leads come from. Keep the scoring rules for version 2.
5. **Speed up responses.** Several lost leads went quiet after the first DM. Aim for a reply within 24 hours and a nudge after 3 days, using a set message.

## Decisions Lewis needs to make
- **Deadline:** 20 by the end of January (needs about 3.5 new athletes a month) or by the end of March (the current pace)?
- **Price:** keep £99 for new athletes or move to £129 (an open decision in "Offer v1")? Deciding before January is best.
- **Capacity:** at 20 athletes, do the delivery standards still hold (a 24-hour response on every key session, and a Sunday review of everyone)? If not, decide now what becomes lower-touch.
- **Churn:** how many athletes leave per month? It isn't tracked yet, and it changes the maths.
