# Sales

## What this area is for
Turning interested runners into paying athletes.

## The funnel
1. Instagram (discovery)
2. Enquiry through the Squarespace website form, which arrives in Gmail
3. Consult call on Google Meet
4. Sign-up through a Tally form, which redirects to Stripe for the £99/month subscription
5. Handover to `coaching/` for onboarding

## Ideal client
Ambitious, self-sufficient marathoners who want support and guidance so they can drop the mental load of self-coaching and train more effectively. In practice the roster covers 5K to marathon and mixed abilities. Full ICP: Notion → "ICP: who this is for (and who it isn't)".

## Tools and files
- **Gmail:** new enquiries.
- **Notion:** Leads CRM (Coaching → Leads CRM, with a page per lead and their consult call notes), Consultation Call Template, Referral Ask message template, Corporate Pipeline.
- **Tally:** sign-up form.
- **Google Meet:** consult calls.
- **Skill:** `goal-roadmap`, which builds the A-to-B race roadmap for a consult call.
